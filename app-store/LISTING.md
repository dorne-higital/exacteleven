## Description (4000)

```
Pick a formation. Build your XI. Land the number.

Exact XI is a football line-up puzzle. Your formation is your target: play a 4-4-2 and you're aiming for 442 combined goals and assists. Fill all eleven positions, one slot at a time, choosing from three players whose stats stay hidden until you pick. Hit the number exactly to win. Go over and you've bust.

Miss without busting and you still finish somewhere on the table, from a top-four finish down to relegation, so every game is worth playing to the final whistle.

HOW IT WORKS
• Choose from seven formations, each with its own target
• Every slot offers three real players from England's top flight since 2016/17
• Their goals and assists stay hidden until you commit
• Use a reroll or a hint when the draw goes against you
• Land the total exactly for the win, or bust by going over

DAILY CHALLENGE
• A new challenge every day, the same for everyone
• Keep your daily streak alive

CHALLENGE YOUR MATES
• Create a custom challenge and send the link
• They play the same setup and you compare results

ACHIEVEMENTS AND STATS
• Unlock achievements for wins, streaks and every formation
• Track your record, best finishes and streaks
• Share your result card when you nail it

Real players. Hidden numbers. One exact total.
```
